# AI interactions

Record of the AI-aided pair's use of generative AI. Phase I: Matthias Nagl and Klaus Lex (AI-aided),
Abhirup Mitra and Gaurav Parajuli (human-only, no generative AI). The pairs switch in Phase II.

| file | what |
|---|---|
| [`phase1.md`](phase1.md) | the interactions that shaped the Phase I analysis: prompt, what the AI suggested, what we accepted, changed or rejected and why, plus the failure patterns and practices we took from it |

Tool: Claude (Anthropic), in chat and as a coding agent with access to the repo and the CP servers.

What the record links to:

- `logs/preregistration.md`: every test with what we expected before running it and what came out.
  We chose and wrote down every test before the run, many of them drafted with the AI.
- `logs/decisions.md`: choices that change how numbers are made, with the reason.
- `results/phase1/findings.md`: the first, exploratory pass that the AI wrote up, with the follow-up
  tests it suggested.

Prompts are shortened to what we asked for; each entry also says what we decided.
